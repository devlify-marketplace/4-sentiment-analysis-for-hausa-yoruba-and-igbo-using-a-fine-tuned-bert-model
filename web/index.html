About this model

Fine-tuned Africa-adapted XLM-RoBERTa on the human-annotated NaijaSenti Twitter benchmark.

  • Three labels: negative, neutral, positive.
  • Scores are reported for a named language but do not guarantee that the text is monolingual or interpreted correctly.
  • Dialect, spelling, sarcasm and code-mixing can change results.
Research use only. A prediction is not a fact about its author. Do not use for surveillance, profiling, high-impact decisions or automatic enforcement.
NaijaSenti data are research benchmark material with their own attribution and non-commercial license terms. This local demo does not retain requests. See the project documentation for provenance and limitations.
